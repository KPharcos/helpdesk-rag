# Requesting a Laptop or Hardware

This article covers new laptops, replacements, and accessories such as monitors, docking stations, keyboards and headsets.

## Standard equipment

Every employee receives a standard laptop (14-inch Windows laptop or 14-inch MacBook, chosen by role), a charger, a headset and, for office workers, a docking station and one 27-inch monitor. Developers and designers may request a second monitor and a higher-specification laptop with manager approval.

## Requesting new equipment

1. Open the IT Service Portal at https://itportal.brightfield.example.
2. Choose "Request hardware" and select the item.
3. Add a short business justification. Requests above the standard set need your manager's approval, which the portal requests automatically.
4. Standard items in stock are delivered to your office within 3 working days. Remote employees receive them by courier within 5 working days.

## Laptop replacement and refresh

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. You will receive an email about 30 days before your refresh date with booking instructions. Back up anything stored only locally; files in your OneDrive folder are restored automatically on the new laptop.

## Broken or damaged equipment

Report hardware faults through the IT Service Portal under "Report a problem". For accidental damage (for example a cracked screen) a loan laptop is available the same day at any office IT desk. Liquid damage should be reported immediately; switch the laptop off and do not try to charge it.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk immediately, at any time of day, by phone on the 24/7 urgent line. The number is on the back of your badge and on the intranet home page. IT will lock and remotely wipe the device. Then file a police report if the device was stolen and send the reference number to the IT Service Desk.

## Returning equipment

When you leave Brightfield or receive a replacement, return the old equipment to an office IT desk or use the prepaid courier box sent to remote employees. Do not wipe the laptop yourself.
