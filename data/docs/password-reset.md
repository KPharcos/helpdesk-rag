# Resetting Your Password

Your Brightfield account password is used for your laptop sign-in, email, the intranet, and most internal tools (single sign-on). This article explains how to reset it yourself and what to do if self-service does not work.

## Password rules

- At least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is recommended.
- Must not contain your name or username.
- Must not be one of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 365 days. You get email reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Self-service reset (forgotten password)

1. On any device, open the self-service portal at https://password.brightfield.example.
2. Enter your work email address and select "Forgot password".
3. Approve the verification request in the Authenticator app, or enter the code sent to your registered mobile number.
4. Choose a new password that follows the password rules.
5. On your laptop, connect to the office network or the VPN and sign in with the new password so the cached credentials are updated.

The self-service portal only works if you registered multi-factor authentication (MFA) during onboarding. See the MFA article if you have not.

## Changing a password you still know

On a Windows laptop press Ctrl+Alt+Del and choose "Change a password". On a Mac, open System Settings, then Users & Groups, then select "Change Password". Stay connected to the office network or VPN for a few minutes afterwards so the change synchronises.

## Locked account

After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the self-service portal, which also unlocks the account. Repeated lockouts often come from an old password saved on a phone (email app or Wi-Fi profile); update or remove it there.

## When to contact the IT Service Desk

Contact the IT Service Desk if self-service fails, if you no longer have access to your MFA device, or if you suspect someone else knows your password. For identity verification the agent will ask for your employee ID and will send a one-time code to your manager. IT staff will never ask you to tell them your password.
