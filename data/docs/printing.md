# Printing and Scanning

Brightfield uses secure "follow-me" printing: you send a job to a single queue and release it at any office printer with your badge.

## Setting up the printer

Company laptops have the "Brightfield-Print" queue installed automatically. If it is missing, install "Brightfield Print Client" from the Company Portal (Windows) or Self Service (Mac) and restart the laptop.

## Printing a document

1. Print as normal and choose the "Brightfield-Print" printer.
2. Go to any office printer.
3. Tap your employee badge on the card reader.
4. Select the jobs to print and press "Print".

Jobs that are not released within 24 hours are deleted. Colour and double-sided options can be changed on the printer screen before releasing the job.

## First-time badge registration

The first time you tap your badge, the printer asks you to sign in with your work email and password to link the badge. If your badge is replaced, you need to link it again.

## Scanning

Tap your badge, choose "Scan to me" and place the document on the glass or in the feeder. The PDF is sent to your work email. Scans over 20 MB are split into several emails.

## Printing from home or from personal devices

Printing to office printers from home works over the VPN; jobs wait in the queue until you release them in the office. Personal devices cannot print to office printers. There is no support for home printers on company laptops beyond installing drivers from the Company Portal.

## Problems

- Job does not appear on the printer: check that you chose "Brightfield-Print" and that you are on the office network or the VPN.
- Paper jam or toner empty: follow the instructions on the printer screen, or report it to the office facilities team.
- Badge not recognised: contact the IT Service Desk to re-link your badge.
