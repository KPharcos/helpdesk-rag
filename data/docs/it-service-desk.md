# Contacting the IT Service Desk

The IT Service Desk is the single point of contact for IT problems and requests at Brightfield.

## How to reach the Service Desk

- **IT Service Portal**: https://itportal.brightfield.example for requests and non-urgent problems. You can follow the status of your tickets there.
- **Chat**: the "IT Help" channel in the company chat, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 CET.
- **Phone**: the number is on the intranet home page. Office hours are Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 CET.
- **24/7 urgent line**: for lost or stolen devices, suspected security incidents and outages affecting many people. The number is on the back of your badge and on the intranet home page.

## Response times

| Priority | Example | First response |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| P1 Critical | Outage affecting a whole office, security incident | 30 minutes, 24/7 |
| P2 High | One person cannot work at all | 2 business hours |
| P3 Normal | Something is broken but there is a workaround | 1 business day |
| P4 Request | New software, hardware, access | 3 business days |

## Writing a good ticket

Include what you were trying to do, the exact error message or a screenshot, when it started, your laptop's asset tag (on the sticker under the laptop) and whether colleagues are affected. Good tickets are resolved faster.

## Remote support sessions

An agent may ask to connect to your laptop with the approved remote support tool. You always have to accept the session yourself and you can end it at any time. Close confidential documents before accepting.

## What IT will never do

IT staff will never ask for your password or MFA codes, never ask you to install remote-control software from an email, and never contact you from a personal email address. Report such requests as phishing.
