# New Starter IT Onboarding and Accounts

This article explains the accounts and access a new Brightfield employee gets, and the IT steps for the first week. Managers should also read the section on requesting access.

## Before the first day

The hiring manager submits the "New starter" form in the IT Service Portal at least 10 working days before the start date. IT then creates the account, orders equipment and assigns standard licences. Late requests may mean the laptop is not ready on day one.

## Accounts you receive

- A Brightfield account (username in the form firstname.lastname) used for single sign-on to email, chat, the intranet and most tools.
- An email mailbox and a OneDrive folder for files.
- Access to your department's shared drive and team channels.
- Role-specific systems (for example the CRM or the finance system), requested by your manager.

## First day

1. Collect your laptop and badge from the office IT desk, or open the courier package if you work remotely.
2. Sign in with the temporary password from the welcome email sent to your private address. You must change it at first sign-in; follow the password rules.
3. Register multi-factor authentication within 7 days (see the MFA article).
4. Complete the mandatory security awareness training on the learning platform within your first 14 days.

## Requesting additional access

Access to additional systems or shared drives is requested in the IT Service Portal under "Request access". The request is routed to the system owner for approval. Typical turnaround is 2 working days.

## Changing roles

When you move to another team, your manager requests the new access and removes access you no longer need. Access reviews run every quarter.

## Leaving Brightfield

Accounts are disabled at 18:00 on your last working day. Your mailbox and OneDrive are kept for 30 days and then deleted, unless your manager requests a transfer of the files. Return all equipment as described in the hardware article.
