# Connecting to the Company VPN

The VPN (virtual private network) gives you secure access to internal systems such as the intranet, file shares and finance tools when you work outside the office. Brightfield uses the GlobalConnect VPN client, which is pre-installed on every company laptop.

## Who needs the VPN

You need the VPN for internal-only systems: file shares, the intranet, the HR system and the finance system. Email, chat and video calls work without the VPN. Do not keep the VPN connected for video calls; it slows them down.

## Connecting

1. Make sure you are connected to the internet (home Wi-Fi, mobile hotspot or hotel network).
2. Open GlobalConnect from the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac).
3. Select the portal "vpn.brightfield.example" and click Connect.
4. Sign in with your work email and password, then approve the MFA prompt.
5. The icon turns green when you are connected.

## Common problems

### "Certificate expired" or "Device not trusted"

The VPN only accepts company-managed devices with a valid device certificate. Certificates renew automatically when the laptop is connected to the office network or the VPN at least once every 90 days. If yours has expired, bring the laptop to an office and connect to the "Brightfield-Corp" Wi-Fi for 30 minutes, or contact the IT Service Desk for a remote renewal session.

### Connected but internal sites do not load

Disconnect and reconnect. If that does not help, restart the laptop. Hotel and public networks sometimes block VPN traffic; try a mobile hotspot to confirm.

### Personal devices

Personal laptops and phones cannot use the VPN. Use the web versions of email and chat on personal devices instead.

## Travelling abroad

The VPN works in most countries. Before travelling outside the EU, notify the IT Service Desk at least 5 working days in advance so they can confirm the destination is allowed by the travel security policy.
